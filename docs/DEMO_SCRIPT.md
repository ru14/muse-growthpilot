# Muse GrowthPilot — Three-Minute Demo Script

## Objective

Show the complete product workflow, the technical implementation and the business value
in three minutes. The video is one of the judges' primary artifacts, and in the final
round judges watch it live, so it must make sense without narration from you.

## What the video must earn (judging rubric)

- **Impact (30%):** a real problem and a measurable benefit for small businesses.
- **Meta AI Tooling (25%):** Meta AI visibly doing essential work, beyond a basic call.
- **Technical Depth (20%):** a sound, refined workflow with validation and approval.
- **Demo (25%):** the live product holding up, and a video that is good to watch.

## Demo preparation

- [ ] Start the application before recording.
- [ ] Prepare one Nidhaye product with verified details and an image.
- [ ] Prepare a clean campaign workflow.
- [ ] Verify the approval flow works.
- [ ] Prepare actual or clearly labelled demo metrics.
- [ ] Confirm the recommendation is based on displayed evidence.
- [ ] Close unrelated applications and notifications.
- [ ] Verify the recording is understandable and accessible.

## Recording sequence

### 0:00–0:05 — Required opening line
Say: "Nothing pre-existing. Everything you'll see in GrowthPilot was built during this
hackathon." (Same statement as in SUBMISSION.md. Required by the rules.)

### 0:05–0:25 — The problem
Explain the time and effort small businesses spend creating social campaigns across
platforms and learning what works.

### 0:25–1:10 — AI campaign creation
Add a Nidhaye product and show AI-assisted product understanding. Generate Facebook and
Instagram content. Make it clear which steps Meta AI powers.

### 1:10–1:45 — Human control
Edit the generated content and approve a variant. Explain that approval is required
before anything is published externally.

### 1:45–2:20 — Publishing and measurement
Show the actual publishing or export state. Display the metrics and say where they came
from.

### 2:20–2:50 — Recommendation
Show the next experiment GrowthPilot recommends and the evidence behind it.

### 2:50–3:00 — Technical contribution
Briefly highlight the key code, Meta AI integration or architecture built during the
hackathon.

## Final verification

- [ ] Video opens with the pre-existing work statement.
- [ ] It is a product demo, not slides.
- [ ] Total duration is no more than three minutes.
- [ ] The application shown matches the submitted code.
- [ ] No simulated results are presented as real outcomes.
- [ ] The video link is accessible to the hackathon reviewers (test in a private window).
